# Code of conduct

This repository is where people report issues with the Global Fund Data Service, suggest improvements and help each
other. We want it to be welcoming and useful for everyone: researchers, journalists, partners, developers and
Global Fund staff.

## What we expect

- **Be respectful.** Treat others as colleagues, whatever their background, experience or point of view.
- **Be constructive.** Describe the problem and what would help, not who is to blame.
- **Be patient.** Maintainers and other users give their time here. Not every request can be accepted, and some take
  time.
- **Stay on topic.** Keep issues and discussions about the Data API, the Data Service website and their data.
- **Protect privacy.** Don't post personal data, credentials or confidential information, yours or anyone else's.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory or demeaning language or images
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a conversation
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission
- Spam, advertising, or repeatedly posting the same request
- Any other behaviour that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## How we enforce it

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or delete comments, issues and discussions that break this code of conduct. Depending
on how serious or repeated the behaviour is, they may give a warning, temporarily restrict someone, or block them from
the repository.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, contact the maintainers at [website@theglobalfund.org](mailto:website@theglobalfund.org). You can also
use GitHub's *Report content* option on any comment. Reports are handled in confidence, and we won't take action
against anyone for reporting in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies to everything in this repository: issues, discussions, comments and any other place where you
represent the project.
